Our story

Born on a rainy afternoon.

Monsoon Brew started with a simple idea: a place that feels like the first cup of coffee after the rain. We source beans directly from estates in Chikmagalur and Araku, and roast them every week.

Everything on our menu is made in-house — from the sourdough to the cold brew. Bring a book, bring friends, stay a while.
